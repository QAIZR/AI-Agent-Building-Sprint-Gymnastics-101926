Good questions

Answers before you register.

Do I need to be tech-savvy to do this?

No. If you can use email and a web browser, you can do this. We build live with you, one step at a time, and every session is recorded so you can go back over anything. Plenty of coaches will come in unsure about the tech and leave running their own agents.

What will I need to participate?

A Windows or Mac laptop or desktop you can install software on, with at a minimum 16 GB of RAM and 500 GB of storage. We recommend 24 GB of RAM and 1 TB. This runs real work on your own machine, so a phone, tablet, or Chromebook will not do it, and it is not a place to skimp. You will also need a reliable internet connection, and each person needs at least a Claude Pro subscription, about $20 a month, to build, run, and manage agents. There are real reasons we teach this work in Claude, and there is more on that below. We will tell you exactly what to set up before Day 1.

Are there any additional costs?

The one flat $1,000, with code WCGA, covers the full training for your whole staff, up to five people, or six with your sport administrator. The only other cost is a Claude Pro subscription, about $20 a month per person. That is it, no hidden fees.

Is there anything I need to do before Day 1?

Yes. There is a short round of prework to get you registered and fully set up, your computer ready, Cowork by Claude installed, and your Claude Pro subscription active. Once we begin there is no time to stop and fix tech issues, so our team makes sure every person is prepped and ready before Day 1. Did we mention this is an intensive?!

Are the sessions online or in person?

Everything is virtual. All sessions are live on Zoom, and your Zoom links are posted in Basecamp.

What if I have to miss a day?

You will be fine. Every session is recorded, and the recording and transcript are posted in Basecamp for you to keep. All of your prompts, homework, and assignments live in your Basecamp folders too, so if you miss a session you can watch the recording, grab everything you need, and be caught up for the next day.

Will there be time for questions?

Yes. Each day I teach for an hour and then dismiss the group, but I stick around for a few minutes afterward for a quick Q&A. If something was not clear, stay on and ask.

How is the Sprint run day to day?

We run the whole project through Basecamp. Your prompts, homework, session recordings, and where you submit your work all live there. You and your staff receive your Basecamp invitations once you register and pay.

Can my spouse or a family member join for their own business?

Great idea, and yes. This October cohort is a great fit for it, because you will be in the room with business leaders from other industries anyway, and every tool, agent, method, and prompt we hand you applies to any business leader in any situation. The benefit of the two of you going through it together is real. One thing to be clear on: the WCGA gymnastics offer covers your program's staff, so a spouse or family member joining for a separate, non-gymnastics organization pays the normal rate of $1,000 per person. They cannot come through on the gymnastics deal. If you would like them to join you, have them register and add a note, and I will reach out to them personally to make sure they are fully supported.

Can I share the agents, prompts, tools, and training videos you give me with other people or programs at my school?

Please do not. These systems and tools are our intellectual property, and we have spent hundreds of thousands of dollars building them for you. On top of that, I am discounting this program enormously as an investment in you, your program, and women's gymnastics nationally. If you know someone else who would benefit, I would be grateful if you pointed them to us so they can get the program for themselves. You are on the honor system here, and I trust you. I am proving it by handing you everything.

What if my school will not let me use Claude?

That is a technical question specific to your situation, so let's have a quick side conversation to find the best solution. I am also glad to speak with your administrator or tech team about why these particular tools are needed. Think of it like construction: you need different tools for different reasons. We use saws, hammers, shovels, and chisels because different jobs call for different tools, and it would be extremely challenging to build an entire building with just a shovel. AI tools are much the same, and we will teach you to reach for the right one for each job. For building agents and running them together as a coordinated system, Cowork by Claude is, in our experience, the strongest tool for this work today, and it is what this entire Sprint is built around.
